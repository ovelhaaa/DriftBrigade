# M1.1 listening guide

Download the `DriftBrigade-M1.1-Bakeoff` Actions artifact. Use matched playback gain: files have fixed headroom and were **not** normalized independently. Files 01–06 contain harmonic, plucked/transient, and broadband sections in that order.

## Organic motion (01–06)

- **A — Wander:** listen for wandering/random movement and whether Motion becomes difficult to perceive at high Chaos.
- **B — Paper Narrowband:** listen for whether a clear central rate survives randomness and for any amplitude- or ring-mod-like control character.
- **C — Phase Drift:** listen for a humanized oscillator and whether timing irregularity becomes too obviously “LFO with jitter.”

## Coherence (07–09)

Compare diffuse independent-band motion with metallic/common comb movement. Width is zero and Center is short so stereo decorrelation does not obscure the contrast.

## Filterbank (10–13)

Compare Gentle and Selective at both coherence extremes. Listen for whether Selective clarifies multiscale motion, but also for coloration or obvious band separation.

## Dynamics (14–15)

The source moves quiet → loud → quiet. Listen for level breathing/pumping in Current and whether MotionOnly is more transparent. Neither is selected here.

## Decision checklist

- Organic preference: **A / B / C**
- Preferred bank: **Gentle / Selective**
- Preferred dynamics: **Current / MotionOnly**
- Coherence contrast: **Too weak / Good / Too strong**
